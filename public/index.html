    Theory of Computation Project Overview

    This prototype demonstrates the theoretical foundations and empirical tradeoffs of Regular Languages (Chomsky Type-3). By extracting seven distinct token patterns from real-world web corpora, we compare the declarative convenience of Regular Expressions against the low-level mechanical transparency of a Deterministic Finite Automaton.
